Real-time market intelligence

We build software that watches markets continuously, models how prices move and finds the moments when they disagree — then measures exactly how much of that disagreement can be captured.

Get in touch How it works

Our approach

Watch everything. Model the timing. Act on what lasts.

01

Watch

A cloud platform reads thousands of live prices across markets, in sync, every few seconds — far beyond what any person can follow.

02

Model

Statistical models predict how long each opportunity will last, from its size, timing, market and conditions — and how risky it is to act on.

03

Act

Every signal is confirmed, ranked and sized to the capital available, so effort goes only to the opportunities most likely to still be there.

How it works

From a price change to a ranked signal

  1. 1

    Read

    Live prices from many sources, read in sync

  2. 2

    Match

    The same event recognised everywhere it trades

  3. 3

    Detect

    Price disagreements found in microseconds

  4. 4

    Predict

    Expected lifetime and risk for each one

  5. 5

    Signal

    Confirmed, ranked and sized

Built as twelve independent cloud services on Microsoft Azure in South Africa, covered by hundreds of automated tests. Every price is recorded, so every model can be tested against history before it touches capital.
