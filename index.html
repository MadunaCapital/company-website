01 · The opportunity

Many markets price the same events separately, and their prices drift apart. Spotting a gap is easy. Knowing whether it will last long enough to act on is where the value is.

0+ opportunities found in our first 17-day live test

02 · What we do

Find the opportunity. Know if it will last. Capture it carefully.

01

Find

Our system watches thousands of live prices at once — far more than any person could — and spots the moments when they disagree.

02

Judge

Most opportunities vanish in seconds. Learning from our own data, we predict which ones will last long enough to be worth acting on.

03

Capture

We act only on the best ones, sized to the capital we have, and measure every result — so growth is built on evidence, not hope.
