Stage 2 · Crypto markets & Binance

The same engine, in a market that never closes.

The same coins trade on many exchanges at once, around the clock — the exact conditions our technology was built for.

01

Open 24/7

No match schedule: opportunities appear day and night, every day of the year.

02

Many exchanges, one asset

The same coin, priced separately on every exchange — the disagreement our engine looks for.

03

Open price data

Leading exchanges publish their live prices openly, including Binance.

04

Simpler to match

A coin has one standard name everywhere, unlike team names — one of our hardest problems, already solved.

What we’ll measure first

  • How often prices for the same coin disagree across exchanges, and by how much
  • How long those gaps last, using the lifetime models we already have
  • What remains after trading fees and the time it takes to move money between exchanges

We’ll publish projections for crypto once we have measured data, the same way we did for prediction markets.

Stage 3 · Financial markets

Finally, the largest markets of all.

Shares, currencies and other listed instruments trade across many venues and products. The same three steps — find, judge, capture — apply, with the discipline that regulated markets demand.

What carries over

Watching many prices at once, spotting disagreements, predicting how long they last, and measuring every result.

What’s different

Regulated markets require licences and established partners. We will enter through licensed brokers and partners, not around them.
