01 · The thesis

Prices across fragmented markets drift apart for seconds at a time. Finding the gap is easy. Knowing whether it will last is the edge.

0M price updates analysed in our first 17-day live test — without a single restart

02 · Our approach

Watch everything. Model the timing. Act on what lasts.

01

Watch

A cloud platform reads thousands of live prices across markets in sync, so every comparison is made between prices taken at the same moment.

02

Model

Survival models, trained on our own recorded data, predict how long each opportunity will last and how risky it is to act on.

03

Act

Every signal is confirmed, ranked and sized to the capital available, so effort goes only to the opportunities most likely to still be there.
